# Jade HUD 1.0.0

First release.

## New
- Hold ALT over anything in Core Keeper to see its name and icon.
- Shows where it comes from: Core Keeper or the mod that added it.
- Lists everything else on the tile, like the block and ground under an ore.
- Shows plant growth stage and time until the next stage.
- Shows mob health.
- Tall walls are picked when you point at their top.
- Works when you join a server. Nobody else needs the mod.
